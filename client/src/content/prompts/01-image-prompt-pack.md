# Original AI Ready-Made Prompt Pack

**Author:** Prompt Gallery  
**Purpose:** Copy-ready prompts for image generation, photo editing, design, social media, products, characters, environments, and visual effects.

> These prompts are original prompt templates, not verbatim copies of prompts from external libraries. Replace bracketed fields such as `[NAME]` or `[COLOR]` when needed. For image editing, upload the source image first, then paste the prompt.

## How to use this pack

Paste one prompt at a time into your image generator or AI editor. Keep the subject and important identity details unchanged when editing a real person. If the tool supports reference images, attach the reference before submitting the prompt. You can add an aspect ratio such as `vertical 4:5`, `square 1:1`, `landscape 16:9`, or `story 9:16`.

# 1. Photo Editing and Image Transformation

### Prompt 1 — Professional portrait enhancement
Edit the uploaded portrait into a polished professional headshot. Preserve the person’s exact identity, facial structure, expression, skin tone, hairline, and natural age. Improve only the lighting, sharpness, color balance, and background. Use soft studio lighting, a clean warm-gray background, realistic skin texture, subtle catchlights, natural shadows, and premium corporate photography. Do not change the face or create plastic-looking skin.

### Prompt 2 — Background replacement
Replace the background with a softly blurred modern city park during golden hour. Keep the person’s exact face, body position, clothing, pose, proportions, and hairstyle unchanged. Match the direction and color of the original light so the subject belongs naturally in the new environment. Use realistic depth of field, gentle warm sunlight, natural shadows, and high-resolution photography.

### Prompt 3 — Outfit change
Change the subject’s clothing to a tailored deep-navy blazer over a white shirt. Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, body shape, hands, pose, and identity. Make the fabric folds, seams, collar, and shadows physically realistic. Keep the original camera angle and composition. Use clean editorial portrait lighting and a neutral background.

### Prompt 4 — Change time of day
Transform the uploaded daytime scene into a calm blue-hour evening scene. Preserve all buildings, people, objects, and their positions. Add realistic cool ambient light, warm window illumination, subtle reflections, soft shadows, and a deep blue sky. Do not invent unnecessary objects or alter the architecture.

### Prompt 5 — Remove unwanted objects
Remove the selected person, object, or distraction from the image and reconstruct the background naturally. Preserve the main subject, composition, perspective, texture, lighting, and image quality. Make the repaired area seamless with no blur patches, repeated patterns, ghosting, or visible cloning.

### Prompt 6 — Vintage film conversion
Convert the image into a tasteful 1970s editorial film photograph. Preserve the subject and composition exactly. Add gentle warm color grading, muted greens, soft highlights, fine analog grain, subtle halation, natural contrast, and authentic vintage lens character. Avoid excessive scratches, distorted faces, artificial dust, or extreme fading.

### Prompt 7 — Black-and-white conversion
Convert the uploaded image into a dramatic fine-art black-and-white photograph. Preserve all details and proportions. Use rich blacks, luminous highlights, carefully controlled midtones, realistic film grain, and directional light. Keep skin texture natural and avoid crushing important details.

### Prompt 8 — Color restoration
Restore realistic color to the old or faded photograph. Repair scratches, dust, tears, and discoloration while preserving every person’s identity, expression, facial structure, clothing pattern, and original composition. Use historically plausible colors, natural skin tones, restrained contrast, and high-resolution restoration.

Preserve the exact product shape, dimensions, packaging structure, colors, labels, buttons, ports, seams, and material finish from the reference image. Do not invent extra components, change the brand mark, bend straight edges, or create warped packaging.

### Prompt 110 — Realistic photo finish
Use physically believable lighting, accurate contact shadows, natural reflections, coherent depth of field, realistic surface texture, consistent perspective, and high-resolution detail. Avoid oversharpening, waxy skin, excessive HDR, plastic textures, random blur, and artificial-looking edges.
