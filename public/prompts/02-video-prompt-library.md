# Comprehensive AI Video Ready-Made Prompt Library

**Original collection by Manus AI**

This library contains original, copy-ready prompts for text-to-video, image-to-video, and AI video editors. Each prompt is designed as a short production clip that can be generated separately and assembled into a longer video. This approach is more reliable than asking most video models to create a complete ten-minute film in one generation. Current prompting guidance commonly recommends describing the camera movement first, followed by the scene, one primary action, and supporting visual or audio details. [1]

## Universal production instruction

For every prompt below, generate a coherent video clip with stable subject identity, consistent clothing and props, natural physical motion, realistic lighting, clear foreground and background separation, and smooth temporal continuity. Follow the stated duration and aspect ratio. Use positive descriptions of what should appear. If the tool supports sound, generate the specified narration, dialogue, ambience, and music with clear timing. Keep important spoken lines exactly as written and use accurate lip synchronization.

For a longer story, create each numbered prompt as a separate clip and keep the character description, color palette, voice, music theme, and visual style unchanged across the entire sequence. Use 16:9 for YouTube and training videos, 9:16 for Shorts, Reels, TikTok, and Stories, and 1:1 for square social posts.

> **Prompt structure:** camera movement + setting + subject + one primary action + lighting and style + sound or dialogue + duration + aspect ratio.

# Y. Quality-Control Add-On for Any Video Prompt

Append this instruction to any prompt when supported by the video model:

Generate stable temporal motion with consistent faces, hands, clothing, props, and background geometry. Keep the camera movement smooth and physically believable. Maintain correct anatomy, natural eye direction, accurate lip synchronization, coherent shadows, realistic contact with the ground, and consistent scale. Keep all important dialogue exactly as written. Use clean audio with the specified voice, ambience, effects, and music. Keep generated text minimal and reserve important titles, subtitles, logos, numbers, and labels for post-production.
